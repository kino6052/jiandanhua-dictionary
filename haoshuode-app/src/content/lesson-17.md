Hao-shuo-de handles subjective perspective using the native Mandarin coverb construction `{{word:dui4}} ... {{word:lai2}} {{word:shuo1}}` (from the perspective of / to someone).

To connect multiple subjects within standard Mandarin grammar, use `{{word:he2}}`. Multiple actions or states applied to a single subject are sequenced naturally by using the adverbial particle `{{word:ye3}}` (also) instead of structural conjunction markers.
