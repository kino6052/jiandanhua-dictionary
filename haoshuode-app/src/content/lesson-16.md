Hao-shuo-de builds locational settings by treating spatial concepts as absolute noun destinations rather than abstract markers. To express static location, use the coverb **{{word:zai4}}**. To express movement or direction toward a destination, use the universal movement root **dào**.

Both roots follow standard Mandarin spatial syntax:

`Subject + {{word:zai4}} / dào + Target Object + Spatial Noun`

To explicitly express movement currently in progress toward a location (active traveling), combine the progressive aspect marker **{{word:zai4}}-** with the kinetic verb **{{word:qu4}}** and the destination marker **dào** to form the compound unit **{{word:zai4}}-{{word:qu4}}-dào**.

When a spatial noun stands alone without a target object modifying it, it functions as a regular baseline noun block.
